Catalog Code: PRD-01 // Solid Bio-Energy Stream

Biomass Spent Pellets

Pellets made by drying and compacting the fibrous solids left after extraction. Contact AEI to discuss current availability and fuel specifications.

Product Introduction

Pellets made from post-extraction solids

Extraction leaves fibrous plant material behind. AEI dries and mechanically compacts these solids into Biomass Spent Pellets for industrial heating applications.

Pellet size, moisture, ash and calorific value are important when selecting a fuel. Ask us for the current batch information and check that it suits your boiler or heating equipment before ordering.

Material Profile

Key Characteristics

  • Made from fibrous solids remaining after extraction
  • Pressed into cylindrical pellets for handling as a solid fuel
  • Intended for industrial heating applications
  • Fuel properties should be checked against your equipment and operating requirements
Sector Suitability

Typical Industrial Applications

Suitability depends on individual boiler/burner grate design and fuel-blending requirements. Typical industrial evaluation areas include:

APPLICATION 01

Industrial Steam Boilers

Solid biomass firing or co-firing in compatible industrial steam generation plants across process industries.

APPLICATION 02

Thermic Fluid Heaters & Hot Air Generators

Process heating for drying, chemical processing, textile finishing, and agro-processing operations.

APPLICATION 03

Kilns, Furnaces & Brick/Ceramic Units

Supplemental renewable solid fuel input for industrial thermal chambers and solid-fuel furnaces.

APPLICATION 04

Captive Plant Energy Recovery

Used by manufacturing units seeking agro-residue derived solid fuel alternatives within regional logistics radii.
