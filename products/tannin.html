Sector Suitability

Typical Industrial Applications

Dosing concentrations and grade selection depend on specific industrial process chemistry and customer testing:

APPLICATION 01

Vegetable Tanning & Leather Retanning

Wet-drum vegetable tanning and retanning systems imparting fullness, grain tightness, and natural leather character.

APPLICATION 02

Boiler & Industrial Water Treatment

Evaluated in industrial water treatment blends for oxygen scavenging, sludge conditioning, and metal passivation.

APPLICATION 03

Textile Mordants & Dye Fixation

Natural polyphenolic mordanting agent supporting dye affinity and color fastness on cellulosic and protein fibers.

APPLICATION 04

Wood Adhesives, Mud Thinners & Coagulants

Used as a bio-based phenolic building block in specialty resins, viscosity modifiers, and effluent coagulation.
