About AEI Extraction Industries

A manufacturer based in Bengaluru and Gandrajupalli

AEI makes tannin, catechin, non-edible oil and biomass pellets from cashew-processing by-products at its manufacturing facility in Andhra Pradesh.

Company Profile

What we make and where we work

AEI Extraction Industries Private Limited was incorporated on 2 August 2022 (CIN: U15400KA2022PTC164613). The registered office is on CMH Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru. Manufacturing takes place at MSME-CDP Gandrajupalli, Chittoor District, Andhra Pradesh.

The plant processes cashew testa, the thin skin removed from cashew kernels. AEI separates tannin, catechin and non-edible oil from this material, then dries and compacts the remaining solids into biomass pellets.

The company's product range serves industrial buyers working in leather processing, soap making, specialty chemicals and process heating. Technical specifications and supply formats depend on the product and batch; buyers can request current details from the team.

  • Tannin in liquid form for industrial use.
  • Cashew testa paste / catechin for leather and specialty chemical applications.
  • Non-edible soap-grade oil for soap and oleochemical manufacturing.
  • Biomass spent pellets made from post-extraction solids.
How we work

A practical approach to processing

We focus on processing cashew by-products into materials that industrial customers can evaluate for their processes.

01

Extract useful fractions

The plant makes tannin, catechin and non-edible oil from cashew testa for industrial applications.

02

Supply industrial buyers

AEI works with buyers to share product information, discuss applications and confirm commercial requirements.

03

Use the remaining solids

After extraction, the remaining fibrous material is dried and pressed into biomass pellets.

Production overview

From cashew testa to finished products

The Gandrajupalli facility processes the feedstock, separates product streams and prepares finished materials for dispatch.

STAGE 01

Feedstock Sourcing

STAGE 02

Industrial Extraction

Extraction separates the product streams from the prepared cashew testa.

STAGE 03

Refining & Pelletizing

Concentration of liquid extracts/oils alongside drying and high-pressure pelletization of spent solid residues.

Product supply

Availability, packaging and delivery details are confirmed with the buyer.
