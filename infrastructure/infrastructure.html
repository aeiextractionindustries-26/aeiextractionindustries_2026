Operational Workflow

Typical processing steps

The stages below outline a typical extraction and pellet-making process. Contact AEI to confirm which steps and equipment apply to a specific product.

STAGE 01

Raw Material Reception & Sizing

Cashew testa is received, cleaned and prepared for processing.

STAGE 02

Extraction

The prepared material is processed to recover the required product stream.

STAGE 03

Separation

Liquid product streams are separated from the fibrous solids left after extraction.

STAGE 04

Concentration

Where required, liquid product streams are concentrated for their intended form.

STAGE 05

Drying and pelletizing

Remaining fibrous solids are dried and pressed into biomass pellets.

STAGE 06

Order preparation

Packaging and dispatch details are confirmed with the buyer for each order.
