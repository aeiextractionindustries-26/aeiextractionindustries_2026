Sustainable Manufacturing Objectives

Using the remaining cashew solids

After extraction, the fibrous solids are dried and pressed into biomass pellets. This gives the remaining material a use as an industrial fuel.

Cashew testa: The thin skin removed from cashew kernels is used as feedstock for AEI's tannin, catechin and non-edible oil products.

Remaining solids: Fibrous material left after extraction is made into Biomass Spent Pellets for industrial heating applications.
